About His House Church

A community of faith, family, and purpose — rooted in Centurion since 2015, and celebrating 10 years of God's faithfulness.

How we began

Built on a Simple Conviction

His House Church was born from a simple conviction: that the church should feel like home. In 2015, Senior Pastors Musa and Nozuko Mabasa answered a call to plant a church in the heart of Centurion — a house where God dwells and families thrive.

What began as a gathering of faith-filled families has grown into a vibrant, multigenerational community. Every Sunday at 207 Utrecht Avenue, people from Clubview, Centurion, and beyond gather to worship, grow, and discover what it means to walk in God's purposes.

In 2026, we celebrate 10 years of God's faithfulness — a decade of changed lives, strengthened families, and a community that continues to grow in grace.

Why we exist

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To build a house where God dwells and families thrive — equipping every believer to walk in the Dominion mandate of Genesis 1:28. We exist to see families restored, communities transformed, and God glorified in every sphere of life.

Our Vision

A church where every person — from children to elders — discovers their God-given identity and purpose. We envision a growing community of Kingdom-minded families who bring God's grace and dominion into Centurion, Gauteng, and beyond.

Our foundation

What We Believe

The Word of God

We believe the Bible is the inspired, authoritative Word of God — our ultimate guide for faith, family, and life.

Salvation Through Christ

We believe in Jesus Christ — His life, death, and resurrection — as the only path to salvation and restored relationship with God.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the ongoing work and gifts of the Holy Spirit, empowering believers for life, ministry, and purpose.

Family & Community

We believe the family is God's foundational institution — and that a healthy church is a family of families, rooted in love and accountability.

Dominion

We believe every believer is called to exercise godly dominion — bringing God's Kingdom values into every sphere of society.

Prayer & Worship

We believe prayer and Spirit-filled worship are the heartbeat of the church — connecting us to God and releasing His purposes in the earth.

Meet the Pastors

Senior Pastors Musa and Nozuko Mabasa have served together as a ministry couple since founding His House Church in 2015. Their partnership in leadership is a reflection of the very values they teach — family, faith, and walking together in God's purpose.

Pastor Musa brings a bold, Kingdom-focused teaching style anchored in the Dominion mandate. His messages consistently equip believers to take their God-given authority into their homes, workplaces, and communities.

Pastor Nozuko ministers with a deep pastoral heart, nurturing the family culture and spiritual health of the congregation. Together, they have built a church that is both Spirit-led and family-centred — where every person is known, valued, and challenged to grow.
